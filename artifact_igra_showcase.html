IGRA — a walk through
a PSX-rendered forest

One evening, one prompt at a time: an empty Godot project became a low-res, dithered forest you can walk through, with your own footsteps and your own wind in the trees.

Godot 4.7 GDScript PSX Look Free Renard Noir — Foliage Pack Procedural sky shader

Build log

  • Terrain Ground & vegetation. 150×150m plane, noise-textured turf, and ~50 hand-placed trees, bushes and groundcover from a free foliage pack — with the pack's baked-in "thank you for downloading" watermark quietly removed from the texture.
  • Player First-person controller. WASD + sprint + jump, mouse look, and a head-bob that fades smoothly in and out instead of snapping — footsteps now land exactly on the bob's peak, picked from three grass takes so no two steps repeat.
  • Sky Procedural sky shader. Hand-written GLSL: drifting fbm-noise clouds, a directional sun disc and glow, day-to-ground gradient — no texture files, just math.
  • Render PSX-style screen. The whole scene renders into a low-resolution SubViewport via PSX Look Free, for the chunky, dithered look of late-90s 3D — tuned to skip material conversion so alpha-cutout foliage doesn't turn into black blocks.
  • Audio Ambience & footsteps. A looping ambience track under the scene, three randomized grass-footstep takes tied to the player's stride, both mixed well below the noise floor.
  • Repo Shipped to GitHub. Initialized, committed, and pushed — github.com/Alisher17427/igra.

What's running under the hood

CharacterBody3D player

Gravity, jump, sprint toggle, mouse-captured look with pitch clamp.

Synced head-bob

One sine drives camera offset, roll and footstep timing — smoothed, not snapped.

shader_type sky

fbm clouds, sun disc from LIGHT0_DIRECTION, day/ground gradient — no textures.

SubViewport retro pass

PSXScreen renders the level small, then scales it up in sharp pixels.
